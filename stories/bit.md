# Beijing Institute of Technology

`B.Eng. Electronic and Information Engineering · Beijing, China · 2019–2023`

In the autumn of 2019 I stepped off the train into Beijing — a shy boy still smelling of exam paper and small-town certainty, set loose in a city far too large for him. For a while the city simply swallowed me whole. Then, slowly, it taught me how to stand.

BIT is where my foundations were laid. I learned to read the grammar of signals and circuits, of electromagnetic fields and communication systems, and let advanced mathematics quietly rearrange the way I think. Each time a door opened onto another discipline, I stepped through it. Four years there widened my world more than all the years that came before.

They were not easy years. Three of them passed under the long shadow of the pandemic, and some days genuinely hurt. But sorrow moves on, and what it leaves behind is a kind of steadiness — I came out of it quieter, and far stronger than I went in.

Small things still stay with me: the fat, unbothered geese drifting across a frozen Beihu in Liangxiang, the calm geometry of the campus at dusk, a soldering iron warm in my hand late at night, a drone caught on a bright afternoon at Tsinghua.
