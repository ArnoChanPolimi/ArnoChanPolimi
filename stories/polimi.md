# Politecnico di Milano

`M.Sc. Telecommunication Engineering · Milan, Italy · 2024–2026`

By a happy accident I arrived at Politecnico di Milano, in Telecommunication Engineering — my first life beyond China, and every part of it felt improbably right.

I loved it almost at once. The Alps still ambush me on the most ordinary mornings, and Lake Garda unrolls as wide and blue as an inland sea. There is a lightness to the days here; even a heavy week feels easy to carry when the light falls the way it does over Milan and its cathedral of white stone.

The work has lifted me, too. My professors chase every result back to its first principle, my English has grown surer by the month, and I have gathered tools I now reach for without thinking. These days I am deep in a master's thesis on large language models — full of problems I cannot yet solve, which is exactly why I love it. I would rather keep climbing than arrive.
